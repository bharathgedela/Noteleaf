# Noteleaf

Noteleaf is an open-source, local-first notebook, daily task tracker, and Markdown workspace for Windows and macOS. Notes stay on your computer unless you explicitly enable a feature that sends data elsewhere.

## Encrypted backups

Noteleaf saves portable backups to any folder you choose. If Google Drive or OneDrive is installed on your computer, choose a folder inside its locally synced location and the provider's desktop app will sync the encrypted backup normally.

- The complete backup is encrypted on your computer before it reaches the selected folder.
- Noteleaf does not connect to Google Drive or OneDrive and requests no cloud-account permissions.
- Noteleaf does not browse your cloud files.
- Your sync provider may see normal file metadata, but the backup contents remain encrypted.
